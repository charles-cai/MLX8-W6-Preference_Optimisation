## 🕹️ Final Submissions

<aside>
💡 **ROUND 1 (pre-selection) at 2:00pm on Sunday**

</aside>

Make a folder for your team in [**this drive**](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/17Xz8fsd2s3qN08hlKv1DjLJ-hJQlQJ_Z?usp=sharing) and submit:

- **Any code/models/datasets needed to reproduce your submission** (this can also be links to github, links to Hub, etc: whatever lets us quickly access your submission).
- A **2-minute video** presenting your project & pitch
- **Final submission is at 2:00pm on Sunday.**

🚨 Link for submissions 🚨 - https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/17Xz8fsd2s3qN08hlKv1DjLJ-hJQlQJ_Z?usp=sharing

<aside>
💡 **ROUND 2 (final jury) at 3:00pm on Sunday**

</aside>

Structure the pitch and demo however you like, but try to make it as fun and interactive as possible!

**The 5 finalists will be announced at 3:00pm and will present in front of everyone a 5-minute demo of their project.  The judges will then select the winners based on these final pitches.**
